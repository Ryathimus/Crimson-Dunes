# Slice 2 Draft — 1788 Historical Foundation

## Purpose

Create the foundation slice beginning with the First Fleet in 1788, centred on the original magical stowaway or stowaways.

This slice explains how early wizarding arrivals investigate the Indigenous magical world and begin integrating with, hiding within, or influencing the muggle settlement.

## Why This Slice Matters

The 1810 playable timeline should not float in isolation. The founding conditions of Crimson Dunes School should be a consequence of earlier history.

## Slice Scope

This slice should define, at minimum:

- Original magical stowaway identity or group profile.
- How they travelled with or hid among the First Fleet.
- What they initially believed about Australia and magic.
- How they detected or misunderstood Indigenous magical systems.
- How they related to the muggle settlement.
- What actions created long-term world flags.
- How these world flags influence 1810.

## Candidate World Flags

```text
first_fleet_magic_contact
wizarding_stowaway_present_1788
settler_magic_detected
local_magic_observed_not_understood
muggle_settlement_magically_shadowed
first_contact_misinterpretations
fusion_magic_conditions_seeded
crimson_dunes_school_need_emerging
```

## Candidate Encounter Themes

- Concealed wand use aboard ship.
- First detection of land-based magic after arrival.
- Misreading signs as charms, wards, or accidental magic.
- Decision whether to stay hidden among muggles or contact magical locals.
- Conflict between Statute of Secrecy instincts and survival needs.
- Early attempts to map or classify Country through British wizarding assumptions.
