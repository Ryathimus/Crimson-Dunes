#### **Core Definition**[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Finfinite-realm.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSkill%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en "Sign in to edit")

**Skills** are the paths to perfection and greater understanding. Each skill helps a person do one thing and can evolve as a person’s understanding of the skill grows.  
Skills require [Essence](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/Essence "Essence") to purchase, but do not require Essence to evolve. Instead, the completion of Skill Quests is required.[[1]](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/Skill#cite_note-1)

#### **Skill Energy Source**[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Finfinite-realm.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSkill%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en "Sign in to edit")

- Will

#### **Skills Availability**[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Finfinite-realm.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSkill%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en "Sign in to edit")

- Each Character starts with 3 Active and 3 Passive skill slots.
- Skills are gained by purchasing them from the Shop or by utilising skill tomes which drop as rewards.
- Gain 1 additional Row for each T6 skills to a max of 9 Skill Rows (Skill row: 1 passive / 1 active slot)
- Cannot have less than 3 total Skills of one type (Passive or Active) at T8/T9.
- Cannot have more than 6 total Skills of one type (Passive or Active) at T8/T9.
- Total Lifetime Skills: **18**
- Max at one time: Tier 6: **12** | Tier 7: **9** | Tier 8: **9** | Tier 9: **9**

#### **Skills Advancement**[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Finfinite-realm.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSkill%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en "Sign in to edit")

Skills advance in one of two ways.

- Quest Completion. Skills have system quests that focus on a particular aspect of a skill tier. By completing the quest, your skill will advance to the next tier.
- Tier Skipping. By understanding your skill and forcing your will upon it, you can forcibly advance your skill to the next tier or even multiple tiers. By skipping multiple tiers, you lose out on the potential variant of the skill that would have been at that tier.
#### **Tiers of Power**[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Finfinite-realm.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSkill%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en "Sign in to edit")

Skills gain 1 Tier of Power for each T6 skill obtained.

- Tiers of Power cap at 9. T6 skills will no longer grant Tiers beyond that point.

#### **Skill Rarity**[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Finfinite-realm.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSkill%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en "Sign in to edit")

- Rarity affects the starting quality and complexity of a skill at Tier 1.

#### **Progression of Skills**[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Finfinite-realm.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSkill%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&uselang=en "Sign in to edit")

- **Tier 1**: The Starting Skill. Complexity depends on Rarity
- **Tier 2 - Tier 5**: Foundation skills. Able to be guided and evolved.
- **Tier 6**: True Understanding. The first step in strengthening your skills. Able to use all tier variants.
- **Tier 7**: Focused Understanding. Focusing the power of the skill into one version of the skill. Only able to use the Focused variant.
- **Tier 8**: Combined Understanding. Combine 2 Tier 7 skills to form a new skill. Takes effects from both. Any combination of Active/Passive. A new Passive Skill will remove 1 Active Skill Slot and vice versa.
- **Tier 9**: Unknown. Focuses a Tier 8 skill and bends reality to your will.
