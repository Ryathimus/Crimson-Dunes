**Access to Framework:**

One gains access to the [Framework](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/Framework "Framework") when they "mature" which is different from person to person and race to race. For humans generally around the age of 15. At this age they gain access to Essence, allowing the person to start a [Class](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/Class "Class") or [Cultivation](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/Cultivation "Cultivation") Path. Along with this, they can also start learning [Skills](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/Skill "Skill").
