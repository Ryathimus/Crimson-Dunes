> In the beginning, or at least something resembling a beginning, there were only the Three: entities born out of nothing and everything, existing in the void that contained everything.

> —Prologue narration [[1]](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/The_Three#cite_note-:0-1)

**The Three** are the mysterious creators of all that is within the world of the [Infinite Realm](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Realm "Infinite Realm"). With even their existence a secret to most, the Three prefer to set things up from the shadows and observe events in what amounts to an attempt to stave off boredom [[1]](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/The_Three#cite_note-:0-1). Meanwhile, day-to-day management of reality is delegated to Framework Control and the [Dealmaker](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/Dealmaker "Dealmaker").
