This is a list of all the skills that appears on the game. Skills usually appear at certain Tiers or can appear when they meet a certain condition.

A skill being unlocked is displayed within the Event Log.

Skills can be leveled up by training them, requiring one of your action slots for the duration.

The speed at which a skill levels is dependent upon the rate of the skill being actively researched and can additionally be sped up via Focus (Toggle Auto-Focus to do this automatically).

The total amount of experience points required to level up a skill depends upon the level you are trying to reach, and dramatically increases with higher levels.

It is currently most efficient to train only one Skill at a time as Focus and Auto-Focus will only apply to the skill occupying your topmost action slot.
