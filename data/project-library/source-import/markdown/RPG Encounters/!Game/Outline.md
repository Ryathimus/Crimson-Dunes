A game mixed between the UI of [[Theory of Magic]], and the world of [[The Infinite Realm]]

Decision based, encounter-driven story progression.
Interact with the world through Encounters, ie. your character comes across a group of bandits.
- [[Encounter Description]]: You see a group of 4 bandits ahead in a clearing in the forest.
- [[Encounter Decisions]]: Based on your [[Character Profile]] and includes your encounter history and choices made, you have a number of choices of how to handle the encounter.
- [[Encounter Outcome]]: If required, you will make skill check rolls or skill based actions to proceed with the encounter based on your choice.
- [[Encounter Variables]]: Factors may affect your encounter, such as monsters roaming the area, other people, or the environment.
- [[Encounter Rewards]]: After an encounter, you will receive rewards based on the outcome. These will affect your progression.
