# Monsters

Monsters are encountered during [adventures](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Adventure "Adventure"), especially [dungeons](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Dungeons "Dungeons") and [clashes](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Clashes?action=edit&redlink=1 "Clashes (page does not exist)").

Most, if not all, monsters drop some amount of [gold](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Gold "Gold"). Some monsters may drop items, such as [crafting](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Crafting "Crafting") materials, [gemstones](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Gemstones "Gemstones"), or extra gold

*Some monsters on the list may not be implemented in the game or only appear in [The Unstable Spire](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Adventure#The_Unstable_Spire "Adventure") or [The Catacrypts](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Adventure#The_Catacrypts "Adventure"), denoted with "n/a".
