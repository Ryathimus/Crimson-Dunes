The way a character splits their build between the [Focuses](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/Focus "Focus"). Since each [focus influences](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/Focus_Influence "Focus Influence") the psyche of its user, maintaining a balanced build ensures the user is able to stabilize their mind and not go mad.

  
•**_After_ reaching 5 total Tiers of Power, one of the following applies:**

•**Possess Tiers of Power in _two_ Focuses:**

-Secondary Focus can have 2/3 the number of Tiers of the Primary Focus

  

•**Possess Tiers of Power in _three_ Focuses:**

-Add Tiers of Power for Secondary and Tertiary Focuses together

-The sum cannot be greater than Half of the Primary Focus's Tiers of Power

  
•Going over the balance threshold will inflict **[Focus Madness](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/Focus_Madness "Focus Madness")** which causes increasingly severe mental instability that manifests based on your Focus Influence.
