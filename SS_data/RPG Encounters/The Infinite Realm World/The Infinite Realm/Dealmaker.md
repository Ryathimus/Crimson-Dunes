A being crafted from the combined essence of [The Three](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/The_Three "The Three"), the **Dealmaker** is one of the most powerful beings in existence, subordinate only to its makers. Its role appears to involve managing The Chosen's interaction with the [Framework](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/Framework "Framework"), but beyond that, its underlying intentions and roles are still a mystery. Something quite clear, however, is that it does not interact with the [Infinite Realm](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Realm "Infinite Realm") directly - instead preferring to (or perhaps only able to) influence reality through others and the deals they make with it.
