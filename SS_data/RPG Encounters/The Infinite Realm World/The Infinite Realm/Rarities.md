Rarities affect Items, Classes, Paths, and Starting Skills. There are nine stages of Rarities each differentiated by their color.

Initially, newly integrated beings on the old world, cannot choose anything above the Rare Rarity from the [Dealmaker](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/Dealmaker "Dealmaker").

The known Rarities are:  
Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, Relic, Masterwork, and Eternal
