An elusive organisation unknown even to the highly secretive Cabal, the [League](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/League "League")'s precise intentions are as of yet still unclear. They appear to have similar ultimate goals to the Cabal - to help steer the world in a positive direction - but with the resolve to interfere in a more _direct_ manner. [[3]](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/The_League#cite_note-3)

## Members[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Finfinite-realm.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_League%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en "Sign in to edit")

The [League](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/League "League") is a small group, but highly secretive, so each member goes by a codename.

- Sigmund Ottenson, alias 'Command', is the leader of the group and provides direction to its members.
- [Erdania Xi Jhan](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/Erdania_Xi_Jhan "Erdania Xi Jhan"), alias 'Harmony'
- [Selia Ha Jhan](https://infinite-realm.fandom.com/wiki/Selia_Ha_Jhan "Selia Ha Jhan"), alias 'Sorrow'
- Maleatus Enis, alias 'Shuffle'
- Vesterius, alias 'Horn'
- Fenera, alias 'Cloud'
