# Systems – Checks & Degrees of Success

  

Use these to resolve [[Encounter Decisions]] and [[Encounter Outcome]] with transparent outcomes.

  

## Dice & DCs (generic)

- **d20 + Skill/Attribute vs DC**

- **DC Bands:** Easy 8–10, Routine 11–13, Challenging 14–16, Hard 17–19, Extreme 20+

  

## Degrees of Success

- **Critical Success:** ≥ DC + 10 (or natural 20)

- **Success:** ≥ DC

- **Fail:** < DC

- **Critical Fail:** ≤ DC − 10 (or natural 1)

  

## Momentum (Session/Arc Metric)

- Start at 0 each session (or keep across arc).

- +1 per clean victory; −1 per major failure; +1 for clever advantage; −1 for reckless harm.

- Used to gate certain branches (e.g., Mentor offers require Momentum ≥ 2).

  

## Conduct Flags

- Track behaviors: *Merciful*, *Honorable*, *Deceitful*, *Brutal*, *Loyal*.

- Used by NPCs (see [[Systems - Reputation]] and [[NPC - Powerful Figure]]) to decide outcomes.
