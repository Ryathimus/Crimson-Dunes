# Systems – Rewards

  

Rewards drive progression and unlock branches.

  

## Reward Types

- **XP / Advancement Points:** Earned by objectives, not just combat.

- **Gear / Boons:** Items, tools, favors, one-use abilities (e.g., “Sect Sigil – Call Aid Once”).

- **Reputation:** Faction alignment shifts; unlocks NPC outcomes.

- **Narrative Keys:** Intel, maps, confessions, secret routes.

- **Mentorship Tracks:** Access to training, feats, or unique abilities tied to [[NPC - Powerful Figure]].

  

## Reward Scaling

- **Clean Victory:** Full XP + tiered loot + reputation boost.

- **Messy Success:** Partial XP + conditional loot + mixed reputation.

- **Failure with Learning:** Reduced XP + story clue + reputation penalty.
