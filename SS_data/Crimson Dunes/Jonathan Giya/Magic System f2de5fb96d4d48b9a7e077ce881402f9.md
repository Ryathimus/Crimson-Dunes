# Magic System

[Wizarding World Magic System](Magic%20System/Wizarding%20World%20Magic%20System%208482832f407247baa6c01416f0d811d4.md)

A joining between the wizarding world’s magic as of 1788, to the shamanistic magic of the Aboriginal Kurdaitcha.

The Wizarding World magic is primarily INTENT based, and as such quite a few things can be hand-wave explained away when it comes to why things happen.

Joining this with Aboriginal magic is a way of adjusting and messing with the system to fit how I would like it to be, but with enough original elements that make it a believable branch of HP magic.

Potential adaptations:

- Less dependent on a magical foci like a wand.
- Aboriginals are more rooted in ritual based magic.
    - Magically charged ochre.
        - Different designs give the person different enhancements, ranging from basic protection to increasing a rituals intended effect based on the number of people performing.
    - Enchanted instruments increase the ceremonies effects.
        - Clap sticks
        - Didgeridoo
- Language agnostic, intent/meaning focused.
    - The word or incantation is less dependent on the sounds than the persons understanding of that word. If your language specifies Akuna for ‘flowing water’, or Mina for ‘water’, the result is based on your intent. However, once your understanding of that word is solidified into a spell, it is almost impossible to change it. So be careful when assigning words to spells.

Spell structure:

- Incantation - Akuna
- Intent - Make water
- Effect - Produces a stream of water

Disciplines of Learning:

- Ritual/Ceremonial Arts
- Tarka (Bone) Arts - Spells using bone wands
- Coreeda / Dueling Arts
