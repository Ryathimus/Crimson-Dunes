# Crimson Dunes Academy

Motto: “Walytjara malpa, tjungu walytjara katu.” - Take only memories, leave only footprints.

The meaning behind this proverb is that we should leave places better than how we found them, avoid taking anything from them except the memories and experiences that will stay with us forever.

**Name: Crimson Dunes Academy**

**Location:** Nestled amidst the red sands and rugged landscapes of the Australian Outback, Crimson Dunes Academy is concealed by enchantments, making it visible only to those with magical abilities. The school is strategically situated near magical ley lines and surrounded by ancient and mystical Aboriginal sites.

**Architecture:**
The main building of Crimson Dunes Academy takes inspiration from both British wizarding architecture and the natural surroundings. The exterior is made of weathered sandstone and enchanted eucalyptus wood, with large verandas to provide shade from the intense Australian sun. The roofs are adorned with magical symbols that change color with the phases of the moon.

**Classrooms and Common Areas:**
Classrooms are designed with large windows that allow students to appreciate the unique flora and fauna of the Outback. The interior decor includes Aboriginal artwork and dreamcatchers, blending magical learning with the rich cultural heritage of the land. Common areas feature cozy fireplaces and comfortable seating for students to gather and share stories.

**Quidditch Pitch:**
The Quidditch pitch is set against the backdrop of the Outback's vast horizon. The goalposts are constructed from native timbers, and the pitch is surrounded by protective enchantments to keep local magical creatures at a safe distance. Students may spot magical creatures unique to Australia, like Occamy or Bundimun, watching Quidditch matches from the outskirts.

**Magical Creatures Reserve:**
Crimson Dunes Academy boasts a Magical Creatures Reserve where students can study and interact with indigenous magical creatures. The reserve is carefully monitored and protected to ensure the well-being of the creatures and the safety of the students.

**Herbology Gardens:**
The school grounds feature extensive herbology gardens, cultivating magical plants native to Australia. Students learn about the magical properties of local flora, including Bush Mistletoe and Golden Wattle, while honing their herbological skills.

**Wandlore Workshop:**
A special workshop is dedicated to the study and crafting of wands using native Australian wandwoods. Students learn the art of wandmaking while respecting the magical significance of the chosen materials.

**The Dreaming Observatory:**
High atop a hill overlooking the school, the Dreaming Observatory provides a space for students to connect with the magical energies of the land. Here, they can practice meditation, divination, and explore the rich Dreamtime mythology with guidance from knowledgeable professors.
