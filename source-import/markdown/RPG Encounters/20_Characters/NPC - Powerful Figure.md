# NPC – Powerful Figure

  

**Role:** High-skill protector/antagonist with strong agency 

**Faction:** Sect (or independent) 

**Motivation:** Preserve order, recruit talent, eliminate threats

  

## Arrival Triggers

- Arrives during [[Encounter - Inn Attack]] if noise level ≥ 2 ticks OR casualties > 1 OR reputation check passes.

  

## Decision Gates (see [[Systems - Reputation]] and [[Systems - Checks]])

- **Mentor Offer:** Renown ≥ +1, Momentum ≥ 2, Conduct: Honorable/Loyal

- **Sect Invitation:** Renown ≥ 0, Momentum ≥ 1, Conduct not Brutal

- **Destroy You:** Renown ≤ −1 OR collateral harm; Momentum ≤ −1

- **Ignore You:** Ambiguous record; NPC focuses on higher priority

  

## Boons & Consequences

- **Mentor Offer:** Gain “Mentor Training – Tier 1” boon; unlock mentor path encounters.

- **Sect Invitation:** Access to sect facilities; “Sect Sigil – Call Aid Once.”

- **Destroy You:** Fail state or escape branch; lose reputation; possible scar.

- **Ignore You:** No immediate change; minor clue drops.
