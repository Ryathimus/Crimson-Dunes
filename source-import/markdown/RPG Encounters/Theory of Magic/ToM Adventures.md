Adventures can be done in the Adventure Tab and is unlocked once you take a [Job Class](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Classes#Jobs_Tier "Classes").

Each adventure has floors ranging from 1 to 10 000 that can either be [encounters](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Encounters "Encounters") in Locales or battles in Dungeons (Locales may contain battles while Dungeons may have encounters but this is mostly an exception and not the norm).

While doing locales, the player will face [Encounters](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Encounters "Encounters"), which either give you some loot, modifiers and/or [stress](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Stressors "Stressors").

[Encounters](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Encounters "Encounters") automatically progress while you are in a Locale, but most of them will cause Stresses and if any of your Stress reach the maximum threshold, you will be kicked out of the Locale to rest and return when your Stresses have lowered. Your progress is kept as long as you only continue to adventure in one specific Dungeon.

Dungeons are filled with hostile enemies (known as [monsters](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Monsters "Monsters")), where your wizard (and their [minions](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Minions?action=edit&redlink=1 "Minions (page does not exist)")) confront enemies with weapons, armor, and spells, to win rewards available in the [Loot](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Loot?action=edit&redlink=1 "Loot (page does not exist)") tab.

A battle and is complete/lost when either you are dead or all enemies are defeated. Battles do not reset and when you get back to full life, you will re-enter the dungeon where the enemies will have the same health from before you loss. So eventually, you will overcome almost any battles (unless foes have regen and healing spells stronger than your damage output.)

Apart from Dungeons and locales, there is also a Clash category. A Clash is mostly a "Boss" fight with usually 1-3 battles but your progression is not saved when you lose and you have to restart from the start.

**IMPORTANT NOTE**: If you enter another dungeon/locale and have not finished your current one, **IT WILL RESET** your progression. (But will only be shown when you enter it again.)

You can also use the Search bar on the Adventure Tab to find a particular dungeon or locale (useful for searching adventures associated to seasonal events using the event's emoji symbol)
