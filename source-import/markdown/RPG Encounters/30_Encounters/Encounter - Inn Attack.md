# [[Encounter Description]] – Inn Attack (Night Assault)

You are woken by monsters breaking into the Inn. Screams, splintering wood, and smoke fill the halls.

  

## [[Encounter Decisions]]

Choose based on [[Character Profile]]:

- **Hold the line** (Combat / Athletics) – Protect civilians.

- **Evacuate civilians** (Leadership / Persuasion) – Guide groups to exits.

- **Stealth strike** (Stealth / Melee) – Eliminate key threats quietly.

- **Call for help / Rally allies** (Performance / Persuasion) – Raise defenders.

- **Investigate source** (Perception / Arcana) – Find breach or controller.

  

## [[Encounter Outcome]]

Resolve actions via [[Systems - Checks]]:

- **Combat DCs:** 14–16 (Hard if overwhelmed: 17–19)

- **Evacuation DCs:** 12–14; Critical Success doubles evac capacity.

- **Stealth DCs:** 14 base; Darkness grants −2 to perception DCs.

- **Rally DCs:** 12; Success adds “Town Defenders” ally tag (−2 to next combat DC).

- **Investigate DCs:** 13; Success reveals sigils linking to a sect (narrative key).

  

**Degrees of Success** apply. Update **Momentum**: +1 clean victory, −1 major failure.

  

## [[Encounter Variables]]

- **Environment:** Darkness (−2 to enemy Perception), smoke (−1 to ranged), tight hallways (+1 AoE DC).

- **Threats:** Reinforcement clock (6 ticks). At 3 ticks, elite monster arrives.

- **Population:** Panic level increases DCs for Evacuation by +1 per failed attempt.

- **External NPC:** [[NPC - Powerful Figure]] may arrive if noise ≥ 2 or casualties > 1.

  

## NPC Intervention – Branch

When the [[NPC - Powerful Figure]] arrives, evaluate gates:

- **Mentor Offer:** Renown ≥ +1, Momentum ≥ 2, Conduct: Honorable/Loyal → Gain “Mentor Training – Tier 1.”

- **Sect Invitation:** Renown ≥ 0, Momentum ≥ 1, Conduct not Brutal → Gain “Sect Sigil – Call Aid Once.”

- **Destroy You:** Renown ≤ −1 OR collateral harm; Momentum ≤ −1 → Fail/Escape branch.

- **Ignore You:** No immediate change → Proceed to aftermath.

  

## [[Encounter Rewards]]

- **Clean Victory:** Full XP; “Town Defenders’ Favor” (+1 Reputation locally); consumables from grateful patrons.

- **Messy Success:** Partial XP; minor favor; clue to sect activity.

- **Failure:** Reduced XP; reputation penalty; scar or loss; still drop one clue to keep story moving.
